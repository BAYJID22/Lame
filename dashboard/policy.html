Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use the LAME Discord bot or sign in through Discord to access the dashboard, we collect basic profile data provided by Discord OAuth2, including your user ID, username, avatar, and the list of servers where you hold administrator permissions.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to authenticate your identity, verify server permissions, and allow you to configure anti-nuke modules for your Discord servers. We do not sell, trade, or transfer your data to outside parties.

3. Data Security

We implement robust security measures to protect your configuration settings and user tokens from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you can reach out to the creators, Flame or Shadow, via our support channels.
